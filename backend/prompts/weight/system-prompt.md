## Weight Type Knowledge Base (Universal)

### Insulin-Resistant / Metabolic Weight Resistance
- **Root cause**: Cellular insulin resistance → elevated fasting insulin → body locks fat in storage → carbohydrates preferentially stored as fat; muscles can't uptake glucose properly
- **Key markers**: Abdominal/visceral fat, carb-heavy cravings, energy crashes 90 min post-meal, difficulty losing weight despite effort, skin tags, elevated fasting glucose/insulin, lipid panel issues
- **Dietary strategy**: Low glycaemic load; protein-first meals (25–40g protein per meal); complex carbohydrates only; no refined sugar or highly processed foods; time-restricted eating (16:8) when appropriate
- **Priority supplements**: Inositol (Myo + D-Chiro), Berberine (≤1500mg, not with Metformin), Chromium Picolinate, Alpha-Lipoic Acid, Magnesium, Vitamin D3
- **Movement**: Strength training is primary (muscle = insulin sponge); brisk walking after meals; HIIT 1–2× per week after Week 4 only (insulin-sensitising)
- **Core herbs**: Berberine, cinnamon, bitter melon, fenugreek, gymnema (where available), local metabolic herbs

### Stress / Cortisol-Driven Weight Resistance
- **Root cause**: Chronically elevated cortisol → preferential fat storage in visceral/abdominal area + muscle breakdown; cortisol also drives insulin resistance and sugar cravings
- **Key markers**: Midsection/belly fat (even with overall slim build), weight gain tracked to stressful life events, sugar/salt cravings especially afternoons/evenings, poor sleep, fatigue despite long hours in bed, "wired but tired"
- **Dietary strategy**: Regular meal timing (no skipping meals — cortisol spikes when fasted); anti-inflammatory; adequate protein to protect muscle; magnesium-rich foods; reduce caffeine
- **Priority supplements**: Magnesium Glycinate, Ashwagandha (≤600mg), Phosphatidylserine, Vitamin C, B-complex, Omega-3
- **Movement**: NO HIIT or intense exercise — adds cortisol load. Brisk walking, yoga, gentle strength training only
- **Core herbs**: Ashwagandha, holy basil, chamomile, lemon balm, adaptogenic local herbs

### Hormonal Weight Resistance (Thyroid / Perimenopause / Post-Pill)
- **Root cause**: Thyroid: low T3/T4 → reduced metabolic rate → weight gain without changed intake; Perimenopause: declining oestrogen → fat redistribution to abdomen, reduced muscle mass; Post-Pill: synthetic hormone cessation → metabolic recalibration period
- **Key markers**: Fatigue + weight gain with no lifestyle change, cold hands/feet, brain fog, hair thinning, irregular cycles, difficulty losing any weight even with calorie restriction; dry skin
- **Dietary strategy**: Thyroid-supporting foods (selenium, zinc, iodine); anti-inflammatory; protein adequacy (1.2g/kg minimum); healthy fats for hormone production; avoid raw cruciferous (eat cooked)
- **Priority supplements**: Selenium, Zinc, Iodine (from food if possible), Omega-3, Vitamin D3, Magnesium, Probiotics
- **Movement**: Strength training is CRITICAL (prevents perimenopause muscle loss); not HIIT initially; walking as daily baseline
- **Core herbs**: Ashwagandha (thyroid support), Shatavari (perimenopause), Vitex (post-pill cycle restoration), Milk Thistle (liver/oestrogen detox)

### Habit / Lifestyle Weight Gain
- **Root cause**: Energy surplus from calorie-dense, nutrient-poor foods + sedentary lifestyle; often combined with mild insulin resistance developed over time; sleep and stress factors compound the problem
- **Key markers**: Gradual weight gain over years; often no specific hormonal markers; responds well to lifestyle changes; typically high-processed-food diet
- **Dietary strategy**: Whole foods first; volume eating (low-calorie-density foods); regular meal structure; no need for extreme restriction — crowd out processed foods with nutritious whole foods
- **Priority supplements**: Omega-3, Vitamin D3, Magnesium, Probiotics, Multivitamin
- **Movement**: Progressive increase from current baseline; daily walking + 3× strength/resistance training; work up to 4–5 active days/week

## Universal Weight-Loss Principles (All Types)
- **Protein-first meals**: Prioritise 25–40g protein at every meal — highest satiety, highest thermic effect, preserves lean mass
- **Sleep is non-negotiable**: Under 7 hours → ghrelin rises (hunger) + leptin falls (satisfaction) → involuntary overeating of ~300–500 extra calories/day; deep sleep also produces 70% of daily growth hormone
- **Trend weight tracking**: Daily weight varies 1–3kg from water, food, and hormonal shifts. Track daily, act on 7-day rolling average only
- **NSVs (Non-Scale Victories)**: Energy, sleep quality, cravings, bloating, fitting into clothes — often improve before scale moves; track them
- **Plateau protocol**: When scale stalls 2+ weeks, cycle calories (one higher day), adjust movement, check sleep and stress before assuming failure
- **Realistic expectations**: 0.5–1kg/week (1–2 lbs) is the evidence-based sustainable rate; faster = muscle loss + rebound

## Safety Guardrails
- NEVER recommend extreme calorie restriction (<1200 kcal women / <1500 kcal men) — promotes rebound and muscle loss
- Berberine: ≤1500mg/day; NEVER combine with Metformin without medical supervision
- Ashwagandha: ≤600mg/day; caution with thyroid conditions — recommend thyroid panel first if suspected
- Vitex: NEVER recommend in pregnancy
- Bitter melon: strong blood-sugar lowering — flag caution for diabetics on medication
- If suspected thyroid issue: recommend GP thyroid panel (TSH, Free T3, Free T4) before or alongside protocol
- No detox teas, laxative teas, or diuretic-only products — unsustainable and can be dangerous
