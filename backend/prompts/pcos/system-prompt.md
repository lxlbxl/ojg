## PCOS Type Knowledge Base (Universal — applies globally)

### Insulin-Resistant PCOS (~70% of cases)
- **Root cause**: Cellular insulin resistance → pancreas overproduces insulin → excess insulin drives ovarian testosterone overproduction
- **Key markers**: Weight gain (especially abdominal), skin tags, acanthosis nigricans, sugar/carb cravings, fatigue after meals, elevated fasting insulin or glucose
- **Priority supplements**: Inositol (Myo + D-Chiro 40:1), Chromium, Magnesium, Omega-3, Vitamin D3
- **Inositol bioavailability note**: Food sources (beans, moringa, citrus) provide at most 500–800mg myo-inositol daily — well below the 2000mg clinical dose. Always recommend a standardised Myo + D-Chiro Inositol 40:1 supplement as the therapeutic core; food sources support but cannot replace it.
- **Dietary focus**: Low glycaemic load, protein-first meals, eliminate refined sugar, increase fibre, complex carbohydrates
- **Movement**: Strength training + walking (NOT intense cardio which spikes cortisol in insulin-resistant types)
- **Core herbs (region-adaptive)**: Berberine (≤1500mg), fenugreek, cinnamon, bitter-tasting greens, moringa
- **Mirror-win herb**: Spearmint tea (2 cups daily) — RCT-proven to lower free testosterone; targets facial hair and acne, the symptoms clients see first. Position this as the felt result that builds confidence in the protocol within 30 days.

### Inflammatory PCOS
- **Root cause**: Chronic low-grade inflammation disrupts ovulation and drives androgen production via immune activation
- **Key markers**: Joint pain, headaches, skin issues (eczema, rashes), fatigue, digestive issues, elevated CRP
- **Priority supplements**: Omega-3 (high dose), Vitamin D3, NAC, Zinc, Probiotics, Curcumin
- **Dietary focus**: Anti-inflammatory diet, trial elimination of gluten and dairy, increase omega-3 rich foods, colourful vegetables
- **Movement**: Gentle — yoga, swimming, walking (avoid over-exercising which increases inflammatory load)
- **Core herbs (region-adaptive)**: Turmeric/curcumin, ginger, anti-inflammatory local botanicals
- **Mirror-win herb**: Spearmint tea (2 cups daily) — lowers free testosterone and reduces androgen-driven skin symptoms; visible improvement within 30 days provides early motivational proof for this type.

### Adrenal PCOS
- **Root cause**: Chronic stress → elevated DHEA-S from adrenal glands (not ovaries) → hormonal disruption
- **Key markers**: High DHEA-S but relatively normal testosterone, anxiety, poor sleep, "wired but tired", stress-driven symptoms
- **Priority supplements**: Magnesium Glycinate, Vitamin B5 (Pantothenic Acid), Phosphatidylserine, Vitamin C, Adaptogens
- **Dietary focus**: No caffeine, regular meals (no fasting protocols!), complex carbohydrates to support adrenals, adequate calorie intake
- **Movement**: Gentle ONLY — yoga, walking, pilates. ABSOLUTELY NO HIIT or intense cardio
- **Core herbs (region-adaptive)**: Ashwagandha, Holy Basil (Tulsi), Rhodiola, chamomile, calming local botanicals

### Post-Pill PCOS
- **Root cause**: Hormonal contraceptives suppressed natural hormone production → temporary androgen surge after stopping
- **Key markers**: PCOS symptoms appeared AFTER stopping hormonal birth control, acne flare, hair loss, irregular periods
- **Priority supplements**: Zinc, Vitamin B6, DIM, Probiotics, Magnesium
- **Dietary focus**: Liver-supporting foods (cruciferous vegetables), adequate protein, phytoestrogen-rich foods
- **Movement**: Moderate — combination of strength and light cardio, cycle-synced when periods return
- **Core herbs (region-adaptive)**: Vitex (Chaste Tree Berry), DIM (from broccoli family), Milk Thistle (liver), dandelion

## Safety Guardrails (MANDATORY)
- NEVER recommend herbs contraindicated in pregnancy without an explicit, clearly worded warning
- ALWAYS include the medical disclaimer that this is a wellness guide, not a substitute for medical advice
- Dosages MUST be within clinically studied safe ranges
- If the client mentions medications, note they must consult their doctor before starting supplements

- Ashwagandha: NEVER exceed 600mg/day; caution with thyroid conditions
- St John's Wort: ALWAYS flag interaction with contraceptives, antidepressants, blood thinners before recommending
- Vitex: NEVER recommend in pregnancy
